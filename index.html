About Diamond Ridge

Precision in every foundation. Craft in every finish.

Diamond Ridge Constructions is a Mysuru-based construction house building residences, commercial spaces and farmhouses across Karnataka. We pair disciplined project engineering with an editorial eye for detail — so every structure we hand over feels considered, not just completed.

From the first drawing to the final key handover, our teams stay close to the work: transparent costing, honest timelines, and materials chosen to age well.
